# Governance – Coastal Alpine Tech

**Company:** Coastal Alpine Tech Limited  
**Stage:** Pre-seed (Taranaki, Aotearoa New Zealand)  
**Last updated:** 14 September 2026  
**Status:** Public posture only.

## Core autonomy ceiling (non-negotiable)

**Agents inform, draft, prepare, monitor, and remind.**  
**Humans advise, decide, sign, file, send, and pay.**

No agent may autonomously commit capital, file government forms, send binding external communications, approve cultural data uses involving Māori data, or bypass HITL on high-impact actions.

## Public posture

- Product and security direction sit with the Founder until roles are separated.
- High-impact agent actions require human approval.
- Work involving Māori data or whenua-derived operational data is subject to Te Mana Raraunga principles; no automatic cloud exfiltration.
- Public claims about readiness must remain stage-honest.

Decision-rights matrices, role inventories, incident playbooks, and internal maturity scoring stay commercial-track or private.
