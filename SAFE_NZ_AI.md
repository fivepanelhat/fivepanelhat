# Safe NZ AI

**Coastal Alpine Tech Limited**  
Taranaki, Aotearoa New Zealand  
**Page type:** Public operating description for partners, agencies, iwi organisations, and the public  
**Updated:** 30 August 2026  
**Stage:** Pre-seed. Published posture and design controls — not a certification, audit opinion, or Crown endorsement.



Coastal Alpine Tech is a **Safe NZ AI** business. Every product workflow sits on the **CAT Sovereign Governance Layer** before a model is allowed to inform a decision or actuate a tool. Agents draft. Humans decide.

“Safe NZ AI” is CAT’s own operating description. It is **not** a government badge, Algorithm Charter signatory status, Te Puni Kōkiri programme mark, or any other certification.

---

## Why this page exists

Agencies and Māori development organisations are asked to take AI seriously without giving away control of data, narrative, or decision rights. CAT’s answer is not a slogan. It is a named governance layer, a sovereignty overlay drawn from **Te Mana Raraunga**, and a hard Human-in-the-Loop rule.

This page is written so a first conversation with organisations such as **Te Puni Kōkiri** can start on published facts. It does not claim a current relationship, funding, or endorsement from Te Puni Kōkiri or any iwi.

## CAT Sovereign Governance Layer

The Governance Layer is the named control that every CAT AI workflow must pass through.

It does four jobs:

1. Lock the operating principles (Mutual Protection, due diligence orientation, currency, sovereignty, HITL).
2. Run nine named support layers.
3. Take an organisation from evidence of care to a responsible adoption roadmap.
4. Align that work to current New Zealand instruments, including Te Mana Raraunga.

### Locked principles

| Principle | Public meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Mutual Protection** | The client and CAT are both protected. Client data stays isolated. Outputs are informational. CAT does not certify compliance or accept residual risk on the client’s behalf. |
| **Due diligence orientation** | The question is whether reasonable care has been exercised, and where residual risk remains — not fault-finding for its own sake. |
| **Currency** | Obligation maps and standards references are dated. Material regulatory change needs a human before it changes a recommendation. |
| **Sovereignty overlay** | Te Mana Raraunga principles apply to data paths. Local-first. Owner-influenced keys. No silent exfiltration. |
| **HITL** | Agents inform, map, draft, and monitor. Humans approve, decide, sign, file, send, and pay. |

### Nine support layers

| # | Layer | Public role |
| ---: | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Due Diligence Verification | Evidence of care; residual risk; monitoring |
| 2 | Compliance as a Service | Obligation mapping. Informational. Not a certificate |
| 3 | Privacy as a Service | Privacy Act 2020, consent-graph health, Te Mana Raraunga data care |
| 4 | Law as a Service | Obligation mapping and change monitoring. Not legal advice |
| 5 | Security as a Service | Posture and hardening recommendations |
| 6 | Governance as a Service | Decision rights, accountability, oversight |

## Te Mana Raraunga — how CAT aligns

Te Mana Raraunga is the Māori Data Sovereignty Network. CAT does not speak for the Network, for iwi, or for hapū. CAT treats the published principles as an **operating overlay** on every data path that may carry Māori, community, whenua, or kai-related data.

Data in those settings is treated as **taonga**, not as a free training input.

| Principle | What it means here | How CAT designs for it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rangatiratanga** | Authority and control stay with the people who hold the data | Documented decision rights. Preference for processing in Aotearoa. Owner- or jointly-influenced keys on high-sensitivity data. Technical ability to revoke access. No silent transfer of control. |
| **Whakapapa** | Data has relationships and origin | Provenance on sensitive records. Lineage for derived models, embeddings, and reports. Transformations recorded rather than flattened. |
| **Whanaungatanga** | Relationships and obligations between people and collectives | Consent and access designed for organisations and collectives, not only individual click-through. |
| **Kotahitanga** | Collective benefit and purpose | Purpose binding. Use limited to what was authorised. A path for a community to exit or require change. Commercial value from community data is not assumed to be CAT’s to keep. |
| **Manaakitanga** | Care and dignity | High-stakes or public outputs that touch Māori data require a human cultural / ethical review. Deficit framing is treated as a defect. Required disclaimers on health, funding, and cultural content. |
| **Kaitiakitanga** | Guardianship over time | Encryption in transit and at rest. Local-first / edge preference. No silent exfiltration. Audit logs that themselves respect sovereignty rules. |

### What alignment is not

- It is not a claim that CAT is a Māori organisation, an iwi mandate, or a Te Mana Raraunga Network member on the strength of this page.
- It is not permission to train foundation models on community data.
- It is not a substitute for kanohi ki te kanohi engagement with mana whenua on a live deployment.
- It is not a certificate Te Puni Kōkiri, Te Kāhui Raraunga, or any iwi can be said to have issued.

Where a project would touch iwi, hapū, marae, or whenua data, CAT’s own rule is **HITL plus cultural review readiness** before the work proceeds. That review is not performed by a model.

## Te Tiriti o Waitangi

CAT is a private New Zealand company. Te Tiriti is treated as an **architectural requirement**, not a cover sheet.

In practice that means:

- **Rangatiratanga** — CAT does not take decision rights over Māori data by putting it in a model.
- **Partnership** — any live work with Māori organisations is by agreement, with named human authority on both sides.
- **Active protection / kaitiakitanga** — default-deny on export; local-first processing; fail-closed on sovereignty checks.
- **Ōritetanga** — systems are designed so Māori organisations are not offered a weaker privacy or control posture than other customers.

CAT does not claim to discharge the Crown’s Te Tiriti duties. Those remain with the Crown.

## Human-in-the-Loop (non-negotiable)

| Agents may | Humans must |
| :--- | :--- |
| Inform, draft, prepare, monitor, remind | Advise, decide, sign, file, send, pay |

Standing policies cannot bypass high-impact, cultural, health, or irreversible actions. This applies to CAT’s own Founder’s Office (Grok Bot) as well as to customer-facing agents.

## What we will not say — including in a Te Puni Kōkiri conversation

- That CAT is Safe NZ AI **certified**, government-approved, or an Algorithm Charter signatory
- That Te Puni Kōkiri, an iwi, or Te Mana Raraunga has endorsed CAT
- That CAT speaks for Māori, or that a model can substitute for mana whenua
- That public GitHub contains the full commercial method
- That residual risk is zero
- Prices, valuation, or unpublished customer names

Send this URL. Then ask for a conversation, not a decision.
