# Safe NZ AI

**Coastal Alpine Tech Limited**  
Taranaki, Aotearoa New Zealand  
**Page type:** Public operating description for partners, agencies, iwi organisations, and the public  
**Updated:** 14 September 2026  
**Stage:** Pre-seed. Published posture and design controls — not a certification, audit opinion, or Crown endorsement.

Coastal Alpine Tech is a **Safe NZ AI** business. Every product workflow sits on the **CAT Sovereign Governance Layer** before a model is allowed to inform a decision or actuate a tool. Agents draft. Humans decide.

“Safe NZ AI” is CAT’s own operating description. It is **not** a government badge, Algorithm Charter signatory status, Te Puni Kōkiri programme mark, or any other certification.

---

## Why this page exists

Agencies and Māori development organisations are asked to take AI seriously without giving away control of data, narrative, or decision rights. CAT’s answer is a named governance layer, a sovereignty overlay drawn from **Te Mana Raraunga**, and a hard Human-in-the-Loop rule.

This page is written so a first conversation can start on published facts. It does not claim a current relationship, funding, or endorsement from Te Puni Kōkiri or any iwi.

## Te Mana Raraunga

Te Mana Raraunga is the Māori Data Sovereignty Network. CAT does not speak for the Network, for iwi, or for hapū. CAT treats the published principles as an **operating overlay** on every data path that may carry Māori, community, whenua, or kai-related data. Data in those settings is treated as **taonga**, not as a free training input.

Principle-by-principle design methods stay commercial-track. Where a project would touch iwi, hapū, marae, or whenua data, CAT’s own rule is **HITL plus cultural review readiness** before the work proceeds. That review is not performed by a model.
