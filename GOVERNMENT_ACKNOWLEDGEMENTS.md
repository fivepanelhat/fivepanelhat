## 1. Position

Coastal Alpine Tech is a **Safe NZ AI business**: workflows sit on the **CAT Sovereign Governance Layer** before a model informs a decision. CAT acknowledges the Algorithm Charter for Aotearoa New Zealand and the wider New Zealand responsible-AI posture led by Stats NZ, MBIE, the Government Chief Digital Officer (GCDO / DIA), and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner. Systems implement the **spirit** of the Charter — transparency, Te Tiriti-consistent partnership, people-centred engagement, fit-for-purpose data, privacy and human rights, and retained human oversight — together with the Privacy Act 2020 and Te Mana Raraunga principles. “Safe NZ AI” is CAT’s operating description, not a government badge.
