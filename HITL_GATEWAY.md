# HITL Gateway — Human-in-the-Loop posture

**Organisation:** Coastal Alpine Tech  
**Updated:** 14 September 2026  
**Status:** Public posture only. Integration methods and gate matrices are commercial-track.

## Core principle (non-negotiable)

Agents inform, draft, prepare, monitor, and remind.  
**Humans alone** advise, decide, sign, file, send, pay, or actuate high-impact controls.

No system may present agent output as final authority. Standing policies cannot bypass high-impact, cultural, health, or irreversible actions.

## Public posture

- High-impact actions pause for explicit human approval.
- Cultural, Māori-data, and sovereignty-affecting paths require elevated human review.
- Approvals are logged; circumvention is a policy violation.

Risk-tier matrices, concrete actuator inventories, integration endpoints, and example flows stay commercial-track.
