## 25 August 2026 — Sprintit AI (concise report)

### Don't audit. Sprintit.

**Sprintit AI** is Coastal Alpine Tech’s **first commercial entry point** and the **governance-layer readiness component** of the Kiwi Edge stack.

It is a short, structured review of a customer’s **current AI use and readiness for their business** — so CAT can see what is already in place, where the gaps are, and **which CAT solutions are a genuine fit**. The customer gets a clear picture and a ranked next-step list. CAT gets a clean path to the right offer — without opening with product jargon, architecture diagrams, or a hard sell.

| | |
|--|--|
| **What** | A time-boxed **AI readiness sprint** for the customer’s business. Reviews how they use (or plan to use) AI, edge systems, data, and day-to-day process; returns practical findings and prioritised actions. Positions CAT to recommend the right product path. **Not** a formal audit opinion, certification, or legal conclusion. |

| **Why** | Early conversations fail when the first contact is a stack tour. Customers need to understand **their** readiness first. Sprintit does that in plain business language, then maps to solutions. |

### Problems we are solving

1. **Entry without jargon** — Customers should not have to learn sovereign edge, fleets, or HITL on day one. Sprintit starts with *their* business and AI use.
2. **Fit before pitch** — CAT needs a disciplined way to see which products (Founder OS, Byte Size Kai, edge nodes, governance support) actually match the customer’s situation.
3. **“Audit” language blocks engagement** — Inspection branding signals pass/fail and cost. A **sprint** signals partnership and momentum.
4. **Readiness is the product wedge** — Many regional operators know AI matters but not where they stand. A short readiness pass is often the first paid or pilot conversation that sticks.

### How Sprintit AI came about

Sprintit was shaped from real founder–customer conversations. CAT needed a **first entry point** that:

- reviews the customer’s **AI use and business readiness**,
- surfaces gaps and opportunities in plain terms, and
- shows **what solutions CAT can offer** — without bombarding the customer with technical or product jargon.

That review function sits in CAT’s **governance layer**. Productising it as **Sprintit AI** turns internal discipline into a client-facing door: collaborative, time-boxed, and commercial.
