## 24 August 2026 — Sprintit AI

### Don't audit. Sprintit.

**Sprintit AI** is Coastal Alpine Tech’s client-facing readiness product.

> **Tagline:** Don't audit. Sprintit.  
> **Product:** Sprintit AI

A time-boxed, collaborative readiness sprint for New Zealand founders, farms, EDAs, and operators assessing AI, edge systems, or data-sovereign practice. Outputs are practical findings and a ranked action list for the client and their own advisors.

Sprintit AI does **not** issue formal audit opinions, certifications, or legal conclusions.

**Why the name:** “Audit” often signals inspection, pass/fail, and cost — many regional operators disengage. **Sprintit** keeps the work rigorous while framing it as partnership and momentum.

**Commercial boundary:** Tagline and product name are public. Method, rubrics, question banks, and report packs are **commercial delivery only** — not a free playbook on GitHub.
