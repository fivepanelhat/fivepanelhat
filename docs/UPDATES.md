## 30 August 2026 — Founder’s Office and Grok Bot RevOps campaign

The three days after incorporation have been operating days, not architecture days.

Coastal Alpine Tech Limited is live as of **28 August 2026** (Day 0 of the 30/60/90 launch sequence). Since then the work has been to stand up the **Founder’s Office** — the internal operating team around the founder — and to point that office at first revenue, under Human-in-the-Loop rules.

### What we have been doing (28–30 August 2026)

- Closed incorporation and started the post-incorporation legitimacy sequence (bank, IRD, GST decision, books, insurance enquiry). Agents draft. The founder files.
- Named the internal Grok Bot roster as the Founder’s Office (not a public sales floor): Summit (Chief of Staff), Formation, Kaitiaki Grants, Pipeline, Sentinel, Forge, Scout.
- Separated fleets in writing: **Aether** remains the product fleet inside the Kiwi Edge stack. **Grok Bot** is how the company is run.
- Locked the commercial wedge for Days 0–30: Sprintit AI as public entry, CAT Sovereign Governance Layer as the paid offering, Weaver + AquaGuard as the technical proof. Public GitHub stays posture-only.
- Opened a **RevOps campaign** run through Grok Bot / Aether Summit. The campaign produces discovery lists, unsent outreach drafts, a short discovery script, a buyer one-pager, a paid 90-day pilot outline, invoice readiness checks, and contemporaneous R&D logging. Nothing is sent, filed, or invoiced by a bot.
- Restated the payback rule: a bot artefact only counts if the founder can use it within 48 hours to book a conversation, lodge a draft, issue an invoice, or refuse a risky action.

## 25 August 2026 — Sprintit AI (concise report)

### Don't audit. Sprintit.

**Sprintit AI** is Coastal Alpine Tech’s **first commercial entry point** and the **governance-layer readiness component** of the Kiwi Edge stack.

It is a short, structured review of a customer’s **current AI use and readiness for their business** — so CAT can see what is already in place, where the gaps are, and **which CAT solutions are a genuine fit**. The customer gets a clear picture and a ranked next-step list. CAT gets a clean path to the right offer — without opening with product jargon, architecture diagrams, or a hard sell.

| | |
|--|--|
| **What** | A time-boxed **AI readiness sprint** for the customer’s business. Reviews how they use (or plan to use) AI, edge systems, data, and day-to-day process; returns practical findings and prioritised actions. Positions CAT to recommend the right product path. **Not** a formal audit opinion, certification, or legal conclusion. |
| **Who** | Delivered by Coastal Alpine Tech Limited. Buyers: founders, farms, regional operators, EDAs, and SMEs exploring AI without a large internal tech team. |
| **Where** | Aotearoa New Zealand — local-first, Te Mana Raraunga-aware, human-in-the-loop. |
| **When** | Client framing formalised 24–25 August 2026; packaging continues to evolve. |
| **Why** | Early conversations fail when the first contact is a stack tour. Customers need to understand **their** readiness first. Sprintit does that in plain business language, then maps to solutions. |

### Problems we are solving

1. **Entry without jargon** — Customers should not have to learn sovereign edge, fleets, or HITL on day one. Sprintit starts with *their* business and AI use.
2. **Fit before pitch** — CAT needs a disciplined way to see which products (Founder OS, Byte Size Kai, edge nodes, governance support) actually match the customer’s situation.
3. **“Audit” language blocks engagement** — Inspection branding signals pass/fail and cost. A **sprint** signals partnership and momentum.
4. **Readiness is the product wedge** — Many regional operators know AI matters but not where they stand. A short readiness pass is often the first paid or pilot conversation that sticks.

### How Sprintit AI came about

Sprintit was shaped from real founder–customer conversations. CAT needed a **first entry point** that reviews the customer’s **AI use and business readiness**, surfaces gaps in plain terms, and shows **what solutions CAT can offer** — without bombarding the customer with technical or product jargon. That review function sits in CAT’s **governance layer**.

**Tagline:** Don't audit. Sprintit.  
**Commercial role:** First entry point · readiness review · solution-fit path.  
**Method, rubrics, and report packs:** commercial delivery only.
