## Six Charter commitments and CAT controls

| Charter commitment | What CAT publishes as practice |
|---|---|
| Transparency | Audit trails; model version; inference timestamp; plain-English explanation |
| Partnership | Te Mana Raraunga controls; cultural review on High / Critical work |
| People | Pilot engagement; no silent use of community data |
| Data | Local-first; purpose limitation; provenance |
| Privacy, ethics and human rights | Privacy Act 2020; Human Rights Act 1993; fail-closed screening |
| Human oversight | HITL. Agents inform, draft, prepare, monitor, and remind. Humans advise, decide, sign, file, send, and pay. |
