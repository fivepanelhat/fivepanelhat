# Sprintit AI — governance-layer readiness

**Organisation:** Coastal Alpine Tech  
**Tagline:** Don't audit. Sprintit.  
**Updated:** 26 August 2026  
**Status:** First commercial entry point. Method private.

## 1. One-line role

Sprintit AI is the commercial entry to the **CAT Sovereign Governance Layer**: a short, plain-language review of a customer’s current AI use and business readiness, so both sides can see fit before any stack tour.

## 2. Safe NZ AI sentence

Coastal Alpine Tech is a Safe NZ AI business: Sprintit sits on the governance layer so the first paid conversation is about the customer’s readiness, not an unconstrained model.

## 3. Governance layers this component implements

| Layer | How Sprintit uses it |
|---|---|
| Law | Not a formal audit opinion, certification, or legal conclusion. Fair Trading-safe claims only. |
| Sovereignty | Local-first framing; no requirement to send customer data offshore to start. |
| Official NZ AI instruments | Readiness questions track Charter-spirit themes: transparency, human oversight, privacy. |
| Human authority | CAT delivers the sprint. Humans own findings and next steps. |
| Runtime | No silent training on customer material. |
| Claims | “Readiness sprint”, never “audit certified” or “government approved”. |

## 4. Public vs commercial-track

| Public | Commercial-track |
|---|---|

## 5. Language by audience

| Audience | Line |
|---|---|
| Public | A short sprint that looks at how your business uses AI, in ordinary language. Not an audit. |
| Investors | Entry wedge and qualification gate. Converts curiosity into a fit map without leaking the context layer. |
| Partners / EDAs | Governance-layer readiness for regional SMEs and farms. Maps to CAT products only where fit is real. |
| Customers | We start with your operation. You get a clear picture and a ranked next-step list. |

## 6. What we will not say

- That Sprintit is a statutory audit, ISO assessment, or Privacy Commissioner review  
- That completing Sprintit certifies the customer under the Algorithm Charter  
- Method detail, scores, or prices on public GitHub
