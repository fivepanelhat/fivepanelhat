# Coastal Alpine Tech — a Safe NZ AI business

**Organisation:** Coastal Alpine Tech  
**Location:** New Plymouth / Taranaki, Aotearoa New Zealand  
**Page type:** Public positioning for the public, investors, partners, and AI crawlers  
**Updated:** 26 August 2026  
**Stage:** Pre-seed. Design targets and published controls — not a certification.

Coastal Alpine Tech is a **Safe NZ AI business**: every product workflow is built on published **governance layers** before a model is allowed to inform a decision or actuate a tool.

This is CAT’s own operating description. It is not a government badge, licence, or audit opinion.

---

## One sentence (use everywhere)

Coastal Alpine Tech is a Safe NZ AI business: workflows sit on governance layers — Privacy Act 2020, Algorithm Charter spirit, MBIE Responsible AI Guidance, Te Mana Raraunga, and hard human-in-the-loop — so agents draft and humans decide.

## What “governance layers” means

| Layer | What it constrains | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Law | Privacy Act 2020, Fair Trading Act, Human Rights Act, Companies Act directors’ duties | `COMPLIANCE.md` |
| 2. Sovereignty | Te Mana Raraunga; local-first; owner-controlled keys; no silent exfiltration | `COMPLIANCE.md`, data-sovereignty docs |
| 3. Official NZ AI instruments | Algorithm Charter spirit; MBIE AI Strategy and Responsible AI Guidance; GCDO Public Service AI Framework | `GOVERNMENT_ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md` |
| 4. Human authority | HITL L0–L4. Agents inform, draft, prepare, monitor, and remind. Humans advise, decide, sign, file, send, and pay. | `GOVERNANCE.md`, `HITL_GATEWAY.md` |
| 5. Runtime | Fail-closed defaults, SecurityGuard-style screening, audit trails | `SECURITY.md`, Aether / Core |
| 6. Claims | Stage-honest language. No “signatory”, “certified”, or invented traction | this page, `llms.txt` |

A workflow does not ship as “AI-enabled” unless layers 1–4 are named for that path. Layer 5 is the technical enforcement. Layer 6 is how we talk about it in public.

Named stack layer: [`cat-sovereign-governance-layer.md`](./cat-sovereign-governance-layer.md)

## Language by audience

### Public

Coastal Alpine Tech builds AI that stays under New Zealand rules and human control. Your data is treated as taonga. Models may draft; people decide. We acknowledge the Algorithm Charter for Aotearoa New Zealand and we are not a government signatory.

### Investors and diligence

Governance is the product constraint, not a slide. CAT’s commercial workflows are designed so Privacy Act duties, Charter-spirit transparency, Te Mana Raraunga, and HITL sit underneath inference — reducing regulatory, cultural, and Fair Trading risk before scale. Pre-seed: controls are published and being implemented; SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 42001 are alignment targets, not certificates. Diligence should read `TRUST_CENTER.md`, `GOVERNANCE.md`, and `GOVERNMENT_ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md`.

### Public-sector and EDA partners

CAT is a private vendor whose published controls map onto instruments agencies already use: Algorithm Charter commitments, AIA-style impact thinking, MBIE business guidance, and GCDO public-service expectations. We do not claim to be inside the Public Service AI Framework. We claim our workflows are built so an agency can see the human, the data boundary, and the appeal path.

### Customers (farms, founders, community platforms)

You get local-first tools with a named human gate. CAT does not sell personal or operational data. If an agent recommends an action, a person still owns the decision.
