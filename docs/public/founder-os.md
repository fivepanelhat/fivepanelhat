## 3. What Founder OS is

The public repo name is **[NZ-Start-Up](https://github.com/fivepanelhat/NZ-Start-Up)**. In the Kiwi Edge stack it is the **Founder OS**: local digital capacity for New Zealand company formation, compliance hygiene, grants/RDTI discipline, market and GTM drafts, and EDA white-label packaging.

It sits under the CAT Sovereign Governance Layer. Sprintit AI is a client-facing readiness entry that can land a founder or EDA into this OS. Full skill procedures and jurisdiction packs stay in the context layer.

One-liner from the repo: jurisdiction-native founder OS for New Zealand — under hard legal ceilings, without replacing lawyers, accountants, or the founder’s own filing authority.

| Control | How Founder OS uses it |
|---|---|
| Law | Companies Act formation packs; Privacy Act 2020; UEM Act 2007 (no autonomous cold email); FMCA boundary on funding drafts; no tax-agent acts |
| Sovereignty | Te Mana Raraunga aligned; no cultural extraction; HITL for cultural content |
| Official NZ AI instruments | Charter-spirit human oversight; informational only; not a certification |
