# CAT Sovereign Governance Layer

**Organisation:** Coastal Alpine Tech

**Updated:** 14 September 2026

## Direct answer

The **CAT Sovereign Governance Layer** is Coastal Alpine Tech’s named governance offering for New Zealand organisations that need due diligence across AI, data, privacy, security, and oversight — then adopt AI under current New Zealand standards.

It is not a government framework. It is not an Algorithm Charter signatory badge. It does not certify compliance.

Coastal Alpine Tech is a Safe NZ AI business: workflows pass through the CAT Sovereign Governance Layer so agents draft and humans decide.

## Public posture

- Named layer exists and sits across the Kiwi Edge stack.
- Operating stance: Mutual Protection, due diligence orientation, currency, sovereignty overlay (Te Mana Raraunga), and hard Human-in-the-Loop.
- Aligns in spirit to Privacy Act 2020, Algorithm Charter for Aotearoa New Zealand, MBIE Responsible AI Guidance for Businesses, GCDO Public Service AI Framework, and Te Mana Raraunga.
- Sprintit AI is the plain-language public entry. It is not an audit.
