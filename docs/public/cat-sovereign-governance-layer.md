## Direct answer

The **CAT Sovereign Governance Layer** is Coastal Alpine Tech’s named governance offering and architecture layer for New Zealand organisations that need to show due diligence across AI, data, privacy, security and oversight — then adopt AI under current New Zealand standards.

It is not a government framework. It is not an Algorithm Charter signatory badge. It does not certify compliance.

Coastal Alpine Tech is a Safe NZ AI business: workflows pass through the CAT Sovereign Governance Layer so agents draft and humans decide.

## What the CAT Sovereign Governance Layer is

A cross-cutting layer of the Kiwi Edge stack. Every CAT AI workflow, and every client engagement in this suite, must pass through it before a model informs a decision or a finding is issued.

It does four jobs:

1. Lock the operating principles (Mutual Protection, due diligence orientation, currency, sovereignty, HITL).  
2. Run nine named support layers (eight specialists plus the AI Adoption Layer).  
3. Take the customer from evidence of care to a responsible adoption roadmap.  
4. Align that work to New Zealand instruments: Privacy Act 2020, Algorithm Charter for Aotearoa New Zealand (spirit), MBIE Responsible AI Guidance for Businesses, GCDO Public Service AI Framework, and Te Mana Raraunga.

Public GitHub describes posture. Methods, rubrics, templates and priced packs stay commercial-track.

## Locked principles

These apply to every layer, every engagement, and every agent.

### Mutual Protection Protocol

Every service must protect the client and CAT. Client data stays isolated. Outputs are informational. CAT does not accept residual risk on behalf of the client, does not certify compliance, and does not issue formal legal or audit opinions. Fail-closed sovereignty and consent checks apply before sensitive access.

### Due diligence orientation

Not hierarchy. Not fault-finding. The question is: has reasonable and appropriate due diligence been exercised, and where does residual risk remain that a prudent organisation would want to address?

Findings language: Diligence Strength · Diligence Observation · Residual Risk Note · Strengthening Opportunity.

### Currency

Knowledge bases, obligation maps and standards references are versioned, dated, and kept current. Material regulatory updates require human review before they affect recommendations. Clients can see the currency status of the knowledge applied to their environment.

### Sovereignty overlay

Te Mana Raraunga Must controls, local-first preference, owner-influenced keys, no silent exfiltration, and HITL on sovereignty-affecting actions.

### HITL and autonomy ceilings

Agents inform, map, draft and monitor. Humans approve, decide, sign, file and accept residual risk. Standing policies cannot bypass high-impact, cultural, health, or irreversible actions.

**Rule:** Agents inform, draft, prepare, monitor, and remind. Humans advise, decide, sign, file, send, and pay.

## Nine named support layers

Eight specialists plus one synthesising adoption layer. A shared control plane sits underneath.

| # | Layer | Role |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Due Diligence Verification | Foundation. Evidence of care across systems, process, AI, data and controls. Residual risk. Continuous monitoring. |
| 2 | Compliance as a Service | Standards and obligation mapping (industry standards, Algorithm Charter spirit, client policies). Informational. Not a certification. |
| 3 | Privacy as a Service | Privacy Act 2020, Health Information Privacy Code where relevant, consent-graph health, Te Mana Raraunga data controls. |
| 4 | Law as a Service | Legal obligation mapping and change monitoring. Informational only. Never formal legal advice. |
| 5 | Security as a Service | Posture, control effectiveness, threat-informed hardening recommendations. |
| 6 | Governance as a Service | Decision rights, accountability, oversight structures, standing policy registry. |
| 7 | Policy as a Service | Living policy engine: versioning, conflict detection, impact simulation. Enabler for all layers. |
| 8 | Alignment as a Service | Te Tiriti, values, organisational intent, cultural safety. Highest HITL among specialists. |
| 9 | AI Adoption Layer | Culminating gate. Staged adoption roadmap, target posture, responsible use patterns. |

**Adoption versus migration.** Adoption is what is sold: how the organisation responsibly adopts and operationalises AI under New Zealand standards. Migration is a technical workstream inside Adoption when systems, data flows or controls need to change. Migration is not a separate product.

### Shared control plane

Runtime Sovereignty Gate · Consent Graph · Immutable Audit Trail · Evaluation Harness · HITL Protocol · specialist routing · orchestration · RAG knowledge layer · customisable agent runtime · watermarking.

The control plane is how Aether, the harness, and Weaver enforce this layer. It is not sold as a standalone early-phase product.

## Customer journey

1. **Scope** — boundaries, risk profile, relevant standards.  
2. **Full-Layer Due Diligence Verification** — coordinated pass across layers 1–8.  
3. **Synthesis and matching** — Current State vs Current Standard / Necessary vs Forward Opportunity.  
4. **Constructive findings** — strengths, residual risk notes, strengthening opportunities, currency statement.  
5. **AI Adoption Layer** — staged roadmap, target posture, responsible use patterns.  
6. **Ongoing** — continuous monitoring and targeted module support.

Sprintit AI is the plain-language public entry that can precede a scoped Full-Layer pass. It is not an audit.

### Matching logic

Every area examined produces three views:

- **Current State** — evidence-based.  
- **Current Standard / Necessary** — what is reasonably expected now under New Zealand law, guidance and good practice.  
- **Forward Opportunity** — what can be strengthened toward a current-year ready posture.
