# Alignment Evidence — public posture

**Organisation:** Coastal Alpine Tech  
**Updated:** 14 September 2026  
**Status:** Public posture only. Not a scorecard pack, certification, or audit opinion.

## Purpose

Public language for partners and funders stays stage-honest: **designed in accordance with** New Zealand instruments and Te Mana Raraunga principles where relevant — not “fully compliant”, “certified”, or “guarantees Māori data sovereignty”.

## Allowed external language

- “Designed in accordance with Te Mana Raraunga principles”
- “Implements the spirit of the Algorithm Charter through explicit HITL and documented controls”
- “Aligned with the NZ AI Strategy’s responsible adoption posture” (application focus)

## Not allowed without Founder approval and published evidence

- “Fully compliant with…”
- “Certified under…”
- “Guarantees Māori data sovereignty”
- Third-party agent success rates presented as CAT measurements
- Numeric claim tiers, L0–L4 matrices, or internal scorecard inventories on public GitHub
