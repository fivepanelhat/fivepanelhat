# Safe NZ AI Stack Partner

**Claim tier: L1 Designed** — not a certification, partnership endorsement, or national mandate.

## Positioning

Coastal Alpine Tech positions as a **safe NZ AI stack partner**: a local-first, HITL-hard, Te Mana Raraunga–aware edge AI stack that other NZ organisations can adopt or white-label without surrendering data control or inventing governance from scratch.

### One-liner (external)

> Coastal Alpine Tech provides a **sovereign, local-first AI stack for Aotearoa** — edge runtime, agent governance, and primary-sector portals designed so partners keep data, control, and accountability onshore.

### What “safe” means here (operational, not marketing)

| Pillar | Meaning in practice |
|--------|---------------------|
| **HITL** | Agents inform, draft, prepare, monitor, remind. Humans advise, sign, file, send, pay, deploy. |
| **Local-first** | Core paths work offline; non-local models only when explicit and logged. |
| **No silent exfil** | Owner-controlled credentials; no silent third-party data sales. |
| **NZ law posture** | Designed in accordance with Privacy Act 2020 information privacy principles. |
| **Te Mana Raraunga** | Designed in accordance with the principles; cultural review readiness for high-stakes content. |
| **NZ AI safety spirit** | Algorithm Charter / responsible AI guidance awareness; risk-tiered gates. |

### Who the partner message is for

1. Regional EDAs (e.g. Venture Taranaki class) — stack as regional capability
2. Primary-sector pilots — farms, kai resilience, biosecurity nodes
3. NZ founders — shared guards, HITL, offline LLM wiring
4. Procurement-aware buyers — governance language already in-repo

### What we do **not** claim at L1

- “Certified safe” or SOC 2 Type II attested (path exists; attestation is future)
- “Fully Te Mana Raraunga compliant” without iwi-specific agreements
- Existing LOIs, revenue, or national AI partner status
- Agents that run the company or replace human accountability
