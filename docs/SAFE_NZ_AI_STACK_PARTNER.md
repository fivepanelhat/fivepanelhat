# Safe NZ AI Stack Partner

**Updated:** 14 September 2026  
**Status:** Public posture only. Not a certification, partnership endorsement, or national mandate.

## Positioning

Coastal Alpine Tech positions as a **Safe NZ AI stack partner**: local-first, HITL-hard, Te Mana Raraunga–aware edge AI that other New Zealand organisations can evaluate without surrendering data control.

### One-liner (external)

> Coastal Alpine Tech is a Safe NZ AI business — sovereign, local-first edge AI under hard Human-in-the-Loop, so partners keep data, control, and accountability onshore.

### What “safe” means here (operational, not marketing)

| Pillar | Meaning in practice |
|--------|---------------------|
| **HITL** | Agents inform, draft, prepare, monitor, remind. Humans advise, decide, sign, file, send, pay. |
| **Local-first** | Preference for onshore / on-node processing; no silent third-party data sales. |
| **NZ law posture** | Designed in accordance with Privacy Act 2020. |
| **Te Mana Raraunga** | Designed in accordance with the principles where Māori or community data interests apply. |
| **NZ AI safety spirit** | Algorithm Charter / responsible AI guidance awareness. |

### What we do **not** claim on this surface

- “Certified safe” or SOC 2 / ISO 42001 attested (unless a published report exists)
- “Fully Te Mana Raraunga compliant” without specific agreements
- Existing LOIs, revenue, or national AI partner status
- Agents that run the company or replace human accountability
- Internal scorecards, L0–L4 matrices, or private stack inventories
